## ATES
- Source file: `ates/Kokanee Glacier.kmz` (file date 2026-11-23). Avalanche Terrain Exposure Scale zones, paths, decision points, boundary, access road and landmarks as supplied in the KMZ; converted read-only, style colours dropped. Not a substitute for avalanche forecasts or field assessment.
<!-- ates:end -->

<!-- basemap:begin -->
## Basemap vectors
- Lakes and streams: BC Freshwater Atlas (FWA_LAKES_POLY, FWA_STREAM_NETWORKS_SP), BC Data Catalogue via openmaps.gov.bc.ca WFS. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - British Columbia. Retrieved 2026-11-23, clipped by bbox, geometry simplified (streams 5 m).
- Glacier outlines: BC Glacier Based Area 2021 (WHSE_BASEMAPPING.GBA_GLACIERS_SP, SOURCE_YEAR 2021, Landsat 8 30 m / Sentinel-2 20 m), BC Data Catalogue, OGL-BC. Replaces the undated FWA_GLACIERS_POLY (TRIM-era). Outlines are coarse (30 m imagery) and shown with their survey year.
<!-- basemap:end -->

## Satellite imagery (Sentinel-2)
- Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024. Sentinel-2 L2A (B04/B03/B02, 10 m) via Element 84 Earth Search (public STAC/COGs), acquisition 2024-09-05, scenes S2B_11UMQ_20240905_0_L2A, S2B_11UMR_20240905_0_L2A. Fixed true-colour stretch, resampled to Web Mercator JPEG tiles z10-14. Display only (not used for any metric). Copernicus Sentinel data is free and open.
