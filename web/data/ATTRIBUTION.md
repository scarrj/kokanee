## Basemap vectors
- Lakes and streams: BC Freshwater Atlas (FWA_LAKES_POLY, FWA_STREAM_NETWORKS_SP), BC Data Catalogue via openmaps.gov.bc.ca WFS. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - British Columbia. Retrieved 2026-11-23, clipped by bbox, geometry simplified (streams 5 m).
- Glacier outlines: BC Glacier Based Area 2021 (WHSE_BASEMAPPING.GBA_GLACIERS_SP, SOURCE_YEAR 2021, Landsat 8 30 m / Sentinel-2 20 m), BC Data Catalogue, OGL-BC. Replaces the undated FWA_GLACIERS_POLY (TRIM-era). Outlines are coarse (30 m imagery) and shown with their survey year.
