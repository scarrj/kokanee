## ATES
- Source file: `ates/Kokanee Glacier.kmz` (file date 2026-11-23). Avalanche Terrain Exposure Scale zones, paths, decision points, boundary, access road and landmarks as supplied in the KMZ; converted read-only, style colours dropped. Not a substitute for avalanche forecasts or field assessment.

## Basemap vectors
- Lakes, streams and glacier outlines: BC Freshwater Atlas (FWA_LAKES_POLY, FWA_STREAM_NETWORKS_SP, FWA_GLACIERS_POLY), BC Data Catalogue via openmaps.gov.bc.ca WFS. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - British Columbia. Retrieved 2026-11-23, clipped by bbox, geometry simplified (streams 5 m).
- Glacier outlines carry no survey-date attribute in the FWA; `survey_date` is recorded as the TRIM-era default (c. 1980s–90s) and outlines are likely out of date.
